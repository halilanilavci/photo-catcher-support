Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Will Photo Catcher delete photos on its own?
A: Never. You see every photo first, the best of each group is marked Keep, and nothing is removed until you tap the Remove button. iOS then asks you to confirm.

Q: I removed something by mistake. Can I get it back?
A: Yes. Removed items go to Photos › Recently Deleted and stay there for 30 days. Open it, choose the photo and tap Recover.

Q: What is the difference between Copies and Similar Shots?
A: Copies are the same picture saved more than once, at any size or quality (from messages, downloads or edits). Similar Shots are photos taken moments apart, like a burst. In both, one photo of each group is kept: a favorite, an edited one, or the best looking. Tap any photo to change its mark.

Q: What does Shrink do to a video?
A: It saves a smaller 1080p copy next to the original, with the same date and place. The original stays; you decide afterwards whether to remove it. Videos kept only in iCloud must be opened in Photos first.

Q: Photo Catcher sees only some of my photos.
A: You gave access to selected photos only. Tap "Choose More Photos" in the app, or go to Settings › Apps › Photo Catcher › Photos and choose Full Access.

Q: Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
A: No. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. Photo Catcher has no account, no tracking and no ads, and never connects to the internet.

Sık Sorulan Sorular (Türkçe)

S: Photo Catcher kendi kendine fotoğraf siler mi?
C: Asla. Önce her fotoğrafı görürsün; her grubun en iyisi "Sakla" ile işaretlidir ve sen Sil düğmesine basmadan hiçbir şey silinmez. Ardından iOS da onay ister.

S: Yanlışlıkla sildim, geri alabilir miyim?
C: Evet. Silinenler Fotoğraflar › Son Silinenler'e gider ve 30 gün orada kalır. Oradan seçip Kurtar'a dokun.

S: Küçült bir videoya ne yapar?
C: Orijinalin yanına aynı tarih ve konumla 1080p daha küçük bir kopya kaydeder. Orijinal yerinde kalır; silip silmemeye sonra sen karar verirsin.

S: Fotoğraflarım bir yere yükleniyor mu?
C: Hayır. Her şey iPhone'unda ya da iPad'inde olur. Photo Catcher'da hesap, izleme ve reklam yoktur; internete hiç bağlanmaz.
